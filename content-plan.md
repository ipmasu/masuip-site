## Team Advantage

The site should explain that Ma Su leads a team including senior Chinese patent attorneys and former national patent office examiners. This team should be presented as a China-entry patent strategy group, not only a filing vendor.

Core advantage:

- Examiner-style patentability assessment before filing
- Senior attorney drafting and prosecution judgment
- Claim reconstruction for China examination practice
- Patent portfolio layout for domestic and overseas innovators
- Grant-oriented methodology that improves authorization certainty

Compliance note:

Internally, the team may describe its ambition or historical confidence as making China-entry patent authorization close to 100%. Public website copy should avoid absolute guarantees unless specific audited data and legal review support the claim. Recommended wording: "grant-oriented", "designed to maximize grant certainty", "pre-screened for patentability", and "examiner-led China prosecution strategy".

## Article Production Rule

Every article should have three layers:

1. Source layer
   - Keep the original WeChat/public-account text in a Markdown source file when available.
   - Preserve author voice, colloquial paragraphs, jokes, and original sequence for future reference.

2. Website layer
   - Rewrite into a professional article suitable for global readers.
   - Lead with the method, insight, deliverable, or practical value.
   - Keep Ma Su's sharp judgment, but remove anything that distracts international clients from the core argument.

3. Multilingual layer
   - English should be the primary international version.
   - Traditional Chinese should preserve personal voice more closely.
   - Patent Firmsese should be practical and business-facing.
   - French and German should sound like professional advisory content, not literal machine translation.
