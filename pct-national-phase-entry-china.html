Why It Matters

China national phase entry should not be treated as a purely formal deadline.

A PCT application may be technically strong, but China prosecution still depends on claim support, technical contribution, prior-art feature comparison, amendment restrictions, and how the claim hierarchy is positioned. Reviewing these issues before entry helps reduce avoidable office actions and preserve useful commercial scope.
