Response Strategy

The strongest response usually begins before drafting the answer.

Chinese office actions often turn on how the examiner defines the technical problem, compares features, combines references, and reads support in the specification. A useful response should decide whether to argue, amend, preserve fallback positions, or adjust the broader portfolio route.
