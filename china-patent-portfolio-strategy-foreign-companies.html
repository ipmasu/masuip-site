Portfolio Logic

A single patent rarely protects a market strategy.

For China, portfolio value depends on how claims cover product versions, manufacturing steps, key modules, improvement routes, competitor workarounds, and future enforcement or licensing scenarios. We combine patent-map reading, FTO findings, examiner-style claim analysis, and business context.
