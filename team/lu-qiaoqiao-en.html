Trademark Strategy

Why trademark work cannot be reduced to forms.

The decisive points in trademark cases often lie in evidence, reasoning, and timing rather than paperwork alone. Whether a refusal review can succeed, an opposition or invalidation can become persuasive, or a registered mark can survive a non-use cancellation challenge often depends on whether the company has authentic, continuous, and well-managed use evidence.

Policy Planning

Turning policy applications into a company growth roadmap.

Technology project applications are not just collections of documents. They are integrated presentations of R&D capability, IP foundation, financial indicators, organizational management, and industrial direction. Lu helps companies translate policy language into internal action lists so preparation becomes part of daily operation.
