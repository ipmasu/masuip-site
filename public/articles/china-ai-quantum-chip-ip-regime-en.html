China's AI, Quantum, and Chip IP Rules: Future Technology Competition Starts With Rules

A short IP policy essay on China's emerging IP rules for AI, quantum technologies, chips, data, and patent examination.

By Ma Su Team2026-08-28China IP Policy

Chinese version

China is reportedly preparing stronger IP rules for AI, quantum technology, chips, and other future industries, while quantum technology has also been named as a priority in a new cyber and information-technology plan. The trend shows that future technology competition is not only a race in patent numbers, but also a race in rule design.

AI raises questions about protection for training data, model weights, prompt systems, generated content, and the technical character of algorithmic inventions. Quantum technology raises questions about enablement and inventive step when inventions sit between basic research and engineering implementation. Chip technology combines patents, layout designs, trade secrets, and export controls.

Rules affect filing strategy. Clearer examination standards allow companies to turn algorithms, chip structures, quantum control methods, measurement systems, error correction, and data-processing flows into grantable patent texts earlier. Unclear rules may push companies toward secrecy, reducing technical disclosure.

For innovators, the practical step is to build future-technology IP records early: data sources, model versions, experiment parameters, chip-design iterations, quantum-control results, and engineering validation evidence. Rules will evolve, but a strong evidence chain helps a company choose flexibly among patents, trade secrets, and compliance strategies.
