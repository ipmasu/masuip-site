AI-Era Science Innovation Education

Train the ability to create, not just the ability to answer.

Ma Su's science innovation curriculum teaches a complete route from real-world problem discovery to protected outcomes. It is designed for youth education, school programs, innovation camps, and technical-team workshops.

Educational belief

Innovation is a capability that can be practiced.

Students do not need to begin with expensive kits or grand technical systems. They begin by observing life, asking better questions, collecting evidence, reading existing solutions, and making a first rough prototype.

AI role

AI is a teammate, not a replacement.

AI can help brainstorm, organize data, explain patents, simulate judges' questions, and polish reports. But students must still define the problem, verify facts, test prototypes, and make final judgments.

Curriculum route

From problem to protected result.

01

Problem Discovery

Interest profiles, daily inconvenience logs, user interviews, and real-world observation.

02

Evidence Building

Questionnaire design, field research, data analysis, and problem prioritization.

03

Innovation Tools

Patent search, paper transformation, combination method, reverse method, and AI-assisted ideation.

04

Outcome Protection

Prototype, experiment log, report, defense, patent, copyright, trademark, and evidence chain.
