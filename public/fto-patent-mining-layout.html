FTO-driven patent strategy

Turn FTO analysis into design-around routes, invention mining, and patent portfolio layout.

Ma Su Team uses freedom-to-operate analysis not only to identify infringement risks, but also to find technical spaces where clients can redesign products, mine new inventions, and build more useful patent portfolios.

What this service answers

FTO is most valuable when it becomes a next action.

  • Which third-party patents may affect product launch, manufacturing, export, or China market entry?
  • Which claim features create the real freedom-to-operate risk?
  • Which design-around routes are technically realistic and commercially meaningful?
  • Which improvements discovered during FTO can become new patent mining opportunities?
  • How should those opportunities be organized into a patent portfolio layout?

Core workflow

From risk map to patent map.

We read product features, competitor patents, claim scope, legal status, and technical alternatives together. The output is not only a risk list, but a practical route from risk avoidance to invention creation.

Best fit

Product teams, founders, universities, and patent counsel.

This service is useful when a team is preparing China entry, overseas filing, product iteration, financing due diligence, patent layout, or patent counsel collaboration around a real product.

Typical outputs

Clear deliverables for business and technical decisions.

  • Freedom-to-operate risk map by patent, claim, product feature, and legal status
  • Design-around suggestions tied to specific claim limitations
  • Patent mining opportunities created from technical alternatives and product improvements
  • Recommended patent portfolio layout, including priority topics and claim directions
  • Communication memo for founders, R&D teams, investors, or patent counsel
