Why foreign innovators choose us

We improve the odds before the application enters examination.

Foreign applicants often lose claim value in China because the case is filed as a translation project. ShineRed IP reviews the invention through examiner logic before filing, then adjusts claims, arguments, and portfolio layers so technically supportable inventions enter China with a stronger path to grant.

  • Former CNIPA patent examiner judgment applied before filing
  • Early identification of novelty, inventive-step, support, and clarity risks
  • Claim reconstruction around the real technical contribution
  • Office action response logic prepared from the examiner's perspective
  • China patent portfolio design for products, manufacturing, and future improvements
  • Clear English explanations for founders, overseas counsel, and investors
