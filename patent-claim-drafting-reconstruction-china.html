Claim Strategy

Good China claims need both technical precision and amendment room.

Claim drafting for China should clarify the real technical contribution, avoid unsupported overbreadth, preserve fallback layers, and explain technical effects in a way that can survive examination. Claim reconstruction is especially useful when the original foreign filing was not drafted with Chinese prosecution in mind.
