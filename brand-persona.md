# Ma Su Brand Persona for Global Website

## Core Identity

Ma Su is not positioned as a conventional patent agent first. The stronger global-facing identity is:

**An innovation-methodology expert who uses patent information, examiner-grade IP judgment, AI tools, and hands-on science education to help people turn vague ideas into protectable, valuable innovation.**

In Chinese:

**马肃不是普通“知识产权代理人”人设，而是把专利审查经验、专利信息方法论、AI工具、科创教育和成果转化串成闭环的科创创新导师与知识产权战略顾问。**

Ma Su also leads a specialist team that combines senior Chinese patent attorneys with former CNIPA examiners. The team is positioned around a grant-oriented methodology: before filing, they screen, reconstruct, and layout inventions through examiner-style feature comparison, technical problem abstraction, patent information analysis, and portfolio strategy. The public website should present this as a disciplined process that greatly improves grant certainty and portfolio quality, without using risky absolute promises.

## Persona Pillars

1. Former patent examiner perspective
   - Understands how patent examiners compare technical features.
   - Can translate examination logic into better invention mining, drafting, prosecution, and portfolio strategy.

2. Patent information as innovation fuel
   - Treats patent databases as maps of human invention.
   - Uses patent information not only to avoid duplication, but to discover technical gaps, transferable structures, and new combinations.

3. Practical science innovation educator
   - Built a complete route from problem discovery to protected outcomes:
     discovery, investigation, data analysis, patent search, solution generation, prototype, experiment, presentation, IP protection, and real-world value.
   - Believes innovation is not a genius game, but a trainable capability.

4. AI-era innovation coach
   - Uses AI as a collaborator, not a substitute.
   - Teaches people to define problems, provide context, judge outputs, test ideas, and build evidence.

5. Cross-border IP strategy adviser
   - Helps overseas clients and foreign patent firms understand China-side patent strategy.
   - Connects technical invention, patent layout, commercialization, education, and collaboration.

6. Grant-oriented China patent team leader
   - Leads a team including senior Chinese patent attorneys and former national patent office examiners.
   - Uses internal methodology to pre-screen patentability, reshape technical solutions, and design filing portfolios before prosecution begins.
   - Helps overseas applications entering China avoid weak claim sets, unnecessary office action risk, and scattered filing decisions.

## Tone

The website should sound:

- Expert, but not bureaucratic.
- Methodological, but not abstract.
- International, but still recognizably Ma Su.
- Practical and slightly sharp in judgment.
- Focused on real problems, real data, real prototypes, and protectable outcomes.

Avoid:

- Generic agency slogans.
- Empty phrases such as "one-stop IP service" without a method.
- Presenting Ma Su only as a lawyer/agent.
- Making the site feel like a domestic patent agency template translated into English.
- Absolute legal-service promises such as "guaranteed grant" or "100% authorization" in public-facing copy. Use grant-oriented, examiner-led, high-certainty, and pre-screened language instead.

## Core Narrative

The strongest narrative is:

**In the AI era, answers are cheap. The scarce ability is finding the right problem, understanding existing technology, generating defensible technical alternatives, testing them, explaining their value, and protecting the result. Ma Su's work is to teach and advise this full innovation route.**

For international patent work, the parallel narrative is:

**Entering China is not merely a translation-and-filing step. A strong China patent path requires examiner-grade judgment before filing: whether the technical solution is supportable, where the inventive contribution truly sits, how the claims should be reconstructed, and how the portfolio should defend future business space.**

Homepage should lead with Ma Su's method and authority, not company history.

About page should explain the transition:

- CNIPA patent examiner
- IP strategy adviser
- science innovation educator
- author of AI-era practical science innovation curriculum
- builder of youth science innovation capability framework
- partner for global clients entering China IP practice

Article center should be treated as proof of expertise. Articles should be organized by:

- Patent-map guided innovation
- Patent information and invention mining
- AI-assisted innovation
- Youth science innovation education
- China patent practice
- Technology commercialization
- Foreign patent firm cooperation

Service pages should connect to the method:

- For companies: patent information guided R&D and portfolio design
- For foreign patent firms: China-side grant strategy, office action logic, technical analysis, and examiner-style claim reconstruction
- For schools and education partners: AI-era science innovation curriculum and capability assessment
- For medical and technology teams: problem-driven innovation workshops and patent proposal generation

## Public Copy Guidance for Grant Rate Claims

Internal user statement:

团队包括中国资深专利代理人和前国家局审查员，可以保证进入中国的专利授权率接近百分之百。

Recommended public-facing framing:

- "Our China-entry process is grant-oriented from the first review, combining senior patent attorneys and former CNIPA examiner perspectives."
- "We pre-screen and reconstruct cases before filing to maximize grant certainty in China."
- "For technically supportable inventions, our method is designed to bring China prosecution as close as possible to a predictable grant path."
- "Rather than simply translating and filing, we assess patentability, claim structure, technical contribution, and portfolio value before the case enters examination."

Avoid public-facing wording:

- "We guarantee authorization."
- "100% grant rate."
- "All China patents can be granted."
- "Near 100% authorization" unless backed by audited, clearly scoped historical data and legal review.
